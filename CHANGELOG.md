# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Fifteen pixel pets that live on top of your code: orange cat, gray cat, shiba inu, puppy, golden retriever, corgi, bunny, chicken, duckling, mushroom, guinea pig, mouse, slime, bug and roach.
- Your code is the terrain: pets walk along lines, climb indentation, fall through blank lines, and jump across wide gaps of spaces.
- Pets drop in from the top or leap up from the bottom when you scroll, ride along as you edit, and follow you between files.
- Click a pet to pet it (♥). The bug, roach and mouse scurry away instead, and sleeping pets wake with a start.
- Idle life: pets look around, hum, do little hops and take naps, with small speech bubbles.
- Settings for size, frame rate, gap width, reactions and your starting pet.
